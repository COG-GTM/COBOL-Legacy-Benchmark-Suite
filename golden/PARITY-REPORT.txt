  DIV-VALD-I               PORTVALD

  DIV-VALD-A               PORTVALD
                           Legacy 2000-VALIDATE-ACCOUNT tests all 50 bytes of LS-INPUT-VALUE for NUMERIC, so a 10-digit account plus 40 spaces is always rejected. Modernized mode applies 10 digits.
  DIV-VALD-M               PORTVALD
                           Legacy 4000-VALIDATE-AMOUNT bounds are the field's own representable range, so the test never fails and even non-numeric text passes. Modernized mode requires a numeric value within VAL-MIN/MAX.

  DIV-UPDT-UNKNOWN-ACTION  PORTUPDT
